Potluck Docs

A coral plate rim, a white well, three lines of text in it. A plate and a page are the same shape problem, and the rim is what settles which one you are looking at.

Color

Coral#FF5656The rim band. Mark only, never text.
Coral text#D03025Links and accent copy. 5.1:1 on white.
Ink#231F20Text on light, well and ground on dark.
Ink dim#615B59Secondary text. 6.7:1 on white.
Paper#FFFFFFThe well on light, text on dark.
Ground#F2F1F0Page background on light.

Two corals on purpose. #FF5656 reaches only 3.13:1 against white, so it is a mark color, not a text color. Anything carrying words uses #D03025. Tokens are in color/tokens.css and color/tokens.json.

Construction

Grid64 units
Outer radius30
Well radius22
Band8
Bar height4.4
Corner radius2.2
Bar widths30 / 25 / 17
Bar origin x17

The corner radius is exactly half the bar height, so the ends are true half circles. Change the height and the radius moves with it. Bars are left aligned and ragged right, which is what makes them read as text rather than as an icon.

Rules

Clear space

One quarter of the mark's diameter on all four sides. At 64 units that is 16. Nothing enters it, including the page edge.

Minimum size

16px on screen, 6mm in print. Below that the three lines close up into a block.

Never set #FF5656 as text

It fails contrast against white. Any coral carrying words is #D03025.

Never recolor the band

The coral is the brand. No gradients, shadows, glows, strokes or outlines on any part of the mark.

Never mix light and dark

Light versions go on light grounds, dark on dark. Over a photograph, use a filled version instead.

Never distort it

No rotating, skewing, stretching or squashing, and do not move the bar radius independently of the bar height.

Still open: typography

No wordmark yet, and nothing in this package sets type. Once a typeface is chosen, the lockups, social cards and a type scale follow from it.

Potluck Docs is EkLine's open source project. Shared with the parent mark: the coral, the ink, and the concentric circle construction. Not shared: EkLine's pencil, which is the parent's own object. Deliberately different: EkLine cuts every terminal flat, and Potluck's bar ends are rounded.
